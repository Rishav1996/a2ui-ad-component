# Towards AI: Official Medium Story Submission & Editorial Guidelines

*Reference Archive & Editorial Standard for Submitting Stories to Towards AI on Medium.*  
*Source: [Towards AI Submission Guidelines](https://pub.towardsai.net/submit-your-medium-story-to-towards-ai-a4fa7e8b141d)*

---

## 1. Publication Overview & Editorial Focus

Towards AI is one of the leading publications on Medium covering Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, and AI Engineering.

### Core Target Topics:
- **AI Engineering & LLM Architecture**: Building production applications with generative models, AI agents, multi-agent frameworks, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).
- **Technical Explainers & System Design**: Practical breakdowns of emerging protocols, interface standards, inference economics, and benchmark architectures.
- **Machine Learning & Deep Learning**: Real-world implementation details, trade-offs, model evaluations, and deployment pipelines.
- **Tutorials & Research Syntheses**: High-utility walkthroughs with reproducible code snippets and objective technical analysis.

## 2. Submission Requirements & Rules Checklist

### A. Draft Status & Submission Process
- **Unpublished Medium Drafts Only**: Submissions must be submitted as unpublished drafts directly through Medium’s submission system (`...` menu > *Submit to publication* > *Towards AI*).
- **Review Window**: Editorial review typically takes between **24 to 72 hours** (1 to 3 business days).
- **Monetization**: Authors may publish behind the Medium Partner Program (MPP) paywall (retaining 100% of their earnings) or choose to publish freely unpaywalled. Both qualify for publication and distribution.

### B. Story Length & Readability
- **Minimum Reading Time**: Must be a substantive read, typically targeting at least **5 minutes of reading time (~850+ words)**.
- **Structure & Formatting**:
  - Logical progression with clean hierarchical headings (`H2` / `##` and `H3` / `###`).
  - No ALL CAPS in titles or subtitles.
  - Well-formatted code snippets using markdown code blocks with syntax highlighting.
  - Bulleted or numbered lists to break up dense paragraphs.

### C. Digital Accessibility & Link Standards
- **Image ALT Text**: Every image must have clear, descriptive alternative (ALT) text explaining the content of the image for screen readers and accessibility.
- **Discernible Hyperlinks**: Avoid vague link anchor text like "click here" or "source". Anchor texts must be descriptive and discernible (e.g., "[Gartner Research](...)", "[Google A2UI Specification](...)", "[Zenodo DOI](...)").

### D. Image Rights & Attribution
- **Mandatory Attribution**: Every image, diagram, and figure must include clear attribution in the caption below the image:
  - If created by the author: `(Image by author)` or `(Diagram by author)`.
  - If AI-generated: Explicitly state the tool used (e.g., `Image generated with Midjourney by author`).
  - If third-party or royalty-free: Explicitly credit the source and license.

### E. Originality & Plagiarism Policies
- **Strict Plagiarism Policy**: Zero tolerance for plagiarism. All text and code must be original or properly attributed.
- **Quoted Material Cap**: Quoted or copied external text must not exceed **10%** of the total article length.
- **Numbered/Bibliographic References**: Citations for external research, reports, and papers should be clearly linked or referenced in a concluding *References* section.

### F. Promotional & Financial Content Restrictions
- **No Self-Promotional Backlinks or Aggressive CTAs**: Articles should not serve as advertisements, advertorials, or marketing funnels to private web apps or paid products.
- **Prohibited Financial Topics**: Content concerning personal finance, stock market trading, cryptocurrencies, NFTs, trading bots, or investment advice is strictly prohibited.
- **Affiliate Disclosure**: If any authorized affiliate link is included, a clear, prominent disclosure must precede it.

### G. Recommended Medium Tags
Articles should use 5 targeted, highly relevant tags on Medium:
- `Artificial Intelligence`
- `Machine Learning`
- `Software Architecture`
- `Large Language Models`
- `Web Development` (or `Adtech` / `Generative AI`)
